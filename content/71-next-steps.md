# Where to Go Next

You came in curious and you're leaving *capable*. Let's make sure this sticks — because the difference between people who "tried AI" and people who got a real edge is simply **building the habit.**

## The honest truth about getting good

You don't get better at this by reading more. You get better by **reaching for Claude on real work, repeatedly**, until it's reflex. The skill compounds fast.

:::tip The 2-minute rule
For the next two weeks, whenever you start a task that involves **writing, summarizing, analyzing, or organizing**, pause for 2 minutes and ask: *"Could Claude do the first draft of this?"* Nine times out of ten, yes — and you'll save far more than 2 minutes.
:::

## Your 30-day plan

A gentle ramp that turns knowledge into habit:

| Week | Focus | Do this |
|---|---|---|
| **Week 1** | Make chat reflexive | Use Claude for every first draft (emails, docs, summaries). Build one Project for a live workstream. |
| **Week 2** | Connect your world | Wire up the connectors you actually use (Atlassian, Drive, Slack). Run your stand-up & sprint summary from live data. |
| **Week 3** | Industrialize | Turn your 2–3 most-repeated prompts into **Skills**. Notice which ones your teammates would want too. |
| **Week 4** | Scale & automate | Hand a recurring chore to **Cowork** (with a schedule). Sketch (or build) a **plugin** for your team. Try one Claude Code prototype. |

:::concept The compounding loop
> Notice a repeated task → do it with Claude → if it repeats, make it a **Skill** → if your team needs it, make it a **Plugin** → automate it with **Cowork**.

Run that loop a few times and you'll have quietly built a personal automation stack most people don't even know is possible.
:::
