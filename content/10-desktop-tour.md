# The Desktop Tour

Let's make Claude Desktop feel familiar. We'll do a quick scavenger hunt so you know where everything lives. Open the app and follow along.

:::note Interface evolves
Claude ships updates constantly, so a button might sit a few pixels from where this describes, or carry a slightly different label. The *concepts* below are stable — if you can't find something, the in-app **Search** or **Settings** is your friend.
:::

## The five things that matter

### 1. The chat box (bottom)
Where you type. Press **Enter** to send, **Shift+Enter** for a new line. The paperclip/➕ lets you **attach files**. This is 80% of what you'll use.

### 2. The sidebar (left)
Your history of past chats, plus entry points to **Projects** and **Cowork**. Past conversations are saved here — click any to resume it (Claude still remembers that chat's context).

### 3. The model picker
Usually near the top of a chat or in settings. You'll see names like **Opus**, **Sonnet**, and **Haiku**.

:::concept Which model should I pick?
Think of them as the same teammate at three speeds:

- **Opus** — the most capable; best for hard reasoning, big analysis, tricky writing. A bit slower.
- **Sonnet** — the balanced everyday workhorse; fast and very smart.
- **Haiku** — the fastest; great for quick, simple tasks.

**Default advice:** leave it on the recommended model. Reach for **Opus** when a task is genuinely complex (deep analysis, a thorny spec). You can switch any time.
:::

### 4. Cowork (in the sidebar)
On a paid plan you'll see a **Cowork** entry. This is the agent that does whole jobs on your files. We'll spend all of Module 4 here — just locate it for now.

### 5. Settings
This is where the *power tools* live. Open **Settings** and look for sections like **Connectors**, **Skills**, **Plugins**, and **Directory**. Don't configure anything yet — just confirm you can find them. These are the upgrades we unlock in Modules 3–6.

## The "/" and "➕" menus

Inside a chat, type **`/`** or click the **➕** button. You'll see available **Skills** (and, once you install them, ones from plugins). This is how you *explicitly* trigger a saved capability. Empty for now — you'll fill it up soon.

## Scavenger hunt

Find each of these in your app and tick it off:

- [ ] The **new chat** button
- [ ] The **attach file** (paperclip / ➕) control in the chat box
- [ ] Your **chat history** in the sidebar
- [ ] The **model picker** (Opus / Sonnet / Haiku)
- [ ] The **Cowork** entry in the sidebar (paid plans)
- [ ] **Settings**, and within it: **Connectors**, **Skills**, **Plugins** / **Directory**
- [ ] Type **`/`** in the chat box and see the menu appear

:::tip Two quality-of-life habits
1. **Rename your chats.** A tidy sidebar ("Q3 roadmap brainstorm") beats 40 chats called "New chat."
2. **Start a new chat for a new topic.** Context carries within a chat, so a fresh topic deserves a fresh chat — it keeps Claude focused and your history clean.
:::
